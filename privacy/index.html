Privacy Notice

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Who we are

This website, camr.sa, is run by Camr Studio, an architecture, interior design and urban design studio in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

What we collect

The website has no forms and no accounts, and we don't ask for personal details. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we use what you send only to reply to your enquiry.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are viewed, so we can improve the site. It records things like the pages you visit, how long you stay, your device and browser type, and your approximate location (city or country). We don't use it to identify you, and we don't use it for advertising.

Google Analytics sets these cookies:

  • _ga – tells visits apart; kept for up to 2 years
  • _ga_NMNJ87VZ13 – keeps track of the current visit; kept for up to 2 years

Google processes this data for us. You can read how in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

How to opt out

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The website works the same either way.

Embedded map

The contact section shows a Google Maps map. Loading it connects your browser to Google, which handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Your rights and contact

Under Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL), you can ask us what personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it.

For this or any question about this notice: info@camr.sa
